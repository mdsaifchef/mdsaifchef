Printing, folding, QR setup & social sharing
  • Print at 100% / actual size with browser headers and footers off and background graphics on. Choose matching A4 or A5 paper. Keep important content at least 10 mm from edges; no bleed required.
  • Loyalty cards and reserved tents use A4 only, to preserve cut size and fold alignment. Use 200–300 gsm stock for cards and display signs, 80–120 gsm for handouts. Dashed borders mean cut; the table tent’s center line means fold, not cut.
  • Use Download PNG to save only the artwork, one image per visible page. Choose square or story and 1× quality for a 1080 px-wide social image; 2× (default) or 3× produces sharper, larger files. Allow multiple downloads if prompted. Social formats are for images, not paper printing; choose A4/A5 again before printing.
  • UPI: set the merchant name, ID, phone and local QR image in _data/print_kit.yml. Use the original merchant-issued QR, black on white, without a logo overlay. Leave an unprinted quiet zone of at least four QR modules on every side. The displayed image is 62 mm square on paper, with 8 mm extra white padding. Never stretch, crop or place a fold through it.
  • Before displaying a payment sign, scan the final printed proof using more than one UPI app. Confirm that the recipient and UPI ID match your merchant account and test a small payment. The empty QR area is intentionally not scannable. A shared QR image needs a second device or a UPI app supporting gallery scanning.
